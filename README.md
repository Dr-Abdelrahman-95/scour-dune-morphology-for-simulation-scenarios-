This repository contains the bed morphology data generated in the current study, “**CFD Investigation of Bed Morphology Downstream of a Sluice Gate: Effects of Hydraulic Conditions, Sediment Properties, and Submerged Vanes**.”

The dataset provides bed profiles representing scour and sediment deposition downstream of a vertical sluice gate under different hydraulic conditions, sediment properties, and submerged-vane configurations.

**Dataset Contents**

The dataset covers the following investigation scenarios:

**Gate opening (Yg): 1.0, 2.0, 2.5, and 3.5 cm.**
**Tailwater depth (Yt): 10, 17.5, and 25 cm.**
**Median sediment diameter (D50): 0.10, 0.40, 0.76, and 1.00 mm.**
**Submerged-vane height (Vh): 5, 7.5, and 10 cm.**
**Submerged-vane orientation: 45°, 90°, and 135°.**

**Purpose**
The dataset is intended to support further research into:
Scour-hole development and downstream sediment deposition.
The effects of hydraulic and sediment parameters on bed morphology.
The effectiveness of submerged vanes as a scour-mitigation measure.
CFD model comparison, validation, and further numerical investigations.
Data-driven analysis and machine-learning applications in sediment transport.
